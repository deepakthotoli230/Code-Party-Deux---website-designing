Which events do you cater for? ＋

Wedding and reception events, engagements, birthdays and private parties, corporate gatherings, traditional Kerala catering and custom event catering are listed service categories. Contact the team to discuss your occasion.

Custom menu combinations can be discussed based on your event type, guest count and preferences. Current dishes and menu availability need to be confirmed directly.
